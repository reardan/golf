**139 Word Break** (Medium). Input: `s` on line 1, the dictionary words space-separated on line 2. Output `true`/`false`.
Golf `②ʘW→w①≢⊕0⟦"←w\\20⍳+①≢⊖⌊⟦①\\⊇⟧€⟦\\ʘB⟧⊸⌿≢€+⟧ʘ>ʘ$0≺ṭ` (46 bytes): BFS (`ʘ>`) over the positions `0 … n`; a position `v` steps to `v + |w|` for every word `w` that the 20 letters from `v` (`20⍳+`, clamped to `n-1`, gathered with `⊇€`) start with (`ʘB`); `s` breaks when position `n` is reached. A clamped chunk can only "match" a word that runs past `n`, and `ʘ>` ignores those targets.
Full (s of 200k letters a/b, 40 words of 2–12 letters): `full  golf 0.602s  py 0.165s  OK`.
**Gaps:** `‹slice›` is O(len − i), not O(n): it is `↓` then `↑`, so `①v 20‹slice›` per position made the full input take 21.6 s (repro: `①≢⊕0⟦"①\\20‹slice›_⊕1⍳+⟧ʘ>` on a 200k-letter line, ~35 s); no substring-at-offset compare, so the chunk is gathered by hand; `⊇` takes no list of indices (`5⍳"3⍳⊇` is `index out of range`); `‹slice›` has no digraph. Golf is ~4× slower than Python here (one `ʘB` call per word per position).
