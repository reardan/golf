647 Palindromic Substrings, Medium. Input: one line of lowercase letters (1M random `a`/`b` with one planted 2000-letter palindrome). Output: the count.
Golf `⎕"⊕⧺ℑ→l→s←l≢⊖"⍳+⟦←l⟦&⊘\\-&&-←s\\⊇\\←s\\⊇≡⟧⍷\\_⟧€∑`, 44 bytes: expand around every centre, as `py/` does. `s` is the raw line (with its `\n`) followed by a copy shifted up by one, so the second copy has a different pad on each side and no bounds test is needed; each centre `m` (both parities, `i + j = m`) is mapped to its radius by `⍷` (first `t` where `s[m/2 - t]` and `s[m - i]` differ, stopping there), and the radii are summed. O(n + answer).
Full: `  full  golf 0.290s  py 1.039s  OK`
Gaps: no gather (`⊇` with a list of indices is `index out of range`), so the two lookups per step are per-element `←s\\⊇`; a vector `⊇` would also allow the O(n)-per-level vector form. Prepending one cell to a list (a left pad) has no short spelling (`0ʀ⎕⧺`), hence the shifted second copy. `⍷`'s predicate has to reach the centre through the stack (`&`) or a global, since a block cannot capture a value.
