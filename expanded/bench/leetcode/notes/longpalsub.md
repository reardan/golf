5 Longest Palindromic Substring, Medium. Input: one line of letters and digits (1M random `ab01` with three planted palindromes; edge cases include `0110`). Output: the longest palindrome, the leftmost on a tie.
Golf `⎕"⊕⧺ℑ→l→s←l≢⊖"⍳+⟦←l⟦&⊘\\-&&-←s\\⊇\\←s\\⊇≡⟧⍷⊗\\1$+⟧€"ʘm"→c⊇→k←s←c⊕←k-⊘↓←k⊖↑⊐`, 70 bytes: the `palsubs` centre expansion, but each centre gives `2r + (m&1)` (its length plus one); `ʘm` (`‹argmax›`, first largest) picks the centre, and one `↓↑` cuts the answer out of the first copy of the line. O(n + sum of radii).
