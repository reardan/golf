115 Distinct Subsequences, Hard. Input: two lines, `s` then `t` (letters).
Golf `①②0*⟦②≡&1⍳⊕\\⧺*-1 31≪%⟧⇤ʘ$`, 25 bytes. A fold over the letters of `s` (`①`) of the dp vector D (D[j] = ways to make t[:j+1], starting at zeros, `②0*`): with E = (t ≡ c) (-1 where t has the letter), D' = D - E·([1] ⧺ D) (the shorter length wins), all at once, which is the right-to-left update. `ʘ$` is the last entry. O(|s|·|t|) with vector ops.
Full: `full  golf 0.457s  py 0.591s  OK` (|s| = 3000, |t| = 2550, a planted subsequence, answer < 2^31).
Gaps: **bug-like limit hit**: intermediate counts grow without bound (the answer fits in 32 bits, the dp entries do not), and 64-bit wrap-around is fine for the arithmetic, but a wrapped value in [0x7FFF<<48, 2^63) is taken for a list by the polymorphic `+ - *`, and the program segfaults (minimal: `9223090561878065152 1+Ṅ` segfaults; `distinct9`, 800 a's then b against 400 a's then c (answer 0), crashes the 19-byte version without the mod). Workaround: reduce mod 2^31 every step, `1 31≪%`, 6 bytes. There are no raw elementwise ops, and no short literal for a big modulus.
